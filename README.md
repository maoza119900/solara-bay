Solara Bay is an original open-world crime game: a sun-soaked coastal city to explore on foot and by car, with traffic and pedestrians, cars to steal and drive, a wanted level with police who respond, three story missions, combat, a HUD with a minimap, a start screen and saving. No assets from any commercial game.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080.

### Controls

- WASD / arrows — move on foot, drive the car
- Shift — run
- E — enter, steal or exit a car · pick up a passenger / package
- F — punch
- Mouse aim, click — shoot
- Space — handbrake
- P / Esc — pause · M — mute

### The game

You are Marco "Rook" Reyes, a driver for hire in Solara Bay. Run three story missions — First Fare, Borrowed Wheels, The Big Score — then keep earning with taxi fares and cash drops in free roam. Stealing cars, hitting pedestrians and shooting all raise your wanted level; the Solara Bay PD sends cars and officers after you. Progress and cash save in your browser.
